# PrimeContext Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

PrimeContext contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and community participants are expected to make collaboration respectful, safe, and technically honest. Participation must be free from harassment and discrimination regardless of background, identity, experience, disability, appearance, nationality, religion, or other personal characteristic.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Respect project scope, security boundaries, privacy, and human approval gates.
- Do not pressure anyone to disclose credentials, private repositories, customer information, personal data, or unpublished security details.
- Correct mistakes openly; do not hide failed tests, benchmark exclusions, conflicts of interest, or material limitations.
- Attribute work and respect licensing, copyright, and contribution decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Personal insults, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, or bad-faith review conduct.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Posting secrets, exploit details, private business data, or security reports in public channels.
- Manipulating benchmarks, misrepresenting verification, or claiming performance without supporting evidence.

## Scope

This code applies in repository discussions, reviews, issues, events, private project communications, and public settings where someone represents PrimeContext.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through an approved private channel. Do not include sensitive personal or security information in a public issue. If no reporting channel is published, request a private contact without disclosing the incident details publicly.

Maintainers will limit disclosure to people needed to review the report, consider conflicts of interest, and respond proportionately. Possible actions include a private clarification, warning, removal of content, temporary participation restriction, or permanent exclusion. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in a good-faith review is prohibited.

Security vulnerabilities are handled separately under [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

## Maintainer responsibility

Maintainers must apply this code consistently, document material enforcement decisions privately, protect reporters where possible, and recuse themselves when they cannot review a concern impartially.
